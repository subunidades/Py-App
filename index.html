How these three pillars interact in production

1. ChromaDB breaks documents into overlapping vector chunks and runs a similarity search for your query.

2. LLM (Gemini) synthesizes the retrieved chunks, constrained by a rigid schema.

3. Pydantic validates the JSON. On a ValidationError, a self-correction loop retries with the error trace.
